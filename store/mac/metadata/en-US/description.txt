Beam sends the video you're watching in Safari to your Apple TV.

Right-click a video and choose Beam to Apple TV. Safari's own AirPlay picker opens for that video, you pick your Apple TV, and it plays there.

• Works on page videos even when the site has no AirPlay button of its own, or has turned AirPlay off for the video.
• Works on videos in embedded frames too.
• When a video can't be sent, Beam says why in plain words: the video hasn't loaded yet, it's protected, no Apple TV was found on your network, or the site streams in a way AirPlay can't send (then use Screen Mirroring).
• Never tries to get around protected (DRM) video.

Private by design: Beam collects nothing, has no account and makes no network connections of its own. Your Apple TV is chosen in Safari's AirPlay picker.

To start, open Beam once, then turn it on in Safari Settings > Extensions and allow it on websites. You need an Apple TV (or another AirPlay receiver) on the same Wi-Fi network as your Mac.
