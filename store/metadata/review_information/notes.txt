Beam is a Safari extension for Mac. It needs no account and collects no data.

To test it you need an Apple TV (or another AirPlay video receiver) on the same network as the Mac.

1. Open the Beam app once. It shows whether the extension is on.
2. In Safari, open Settings, then Extensions, and turn on Beam. Choose "Always Allow on Every Website" when Safari asks, or allow it on the site you test with.
3. Open a page with an ordinary HTML video and start playing it.
4. Right-click the page and choose "Beam to Apple TV". Safari's AirPlay list opens; pick the Apple TV.

Protected (DRM) video cannot be sent by AirPlay from an extension. On those pages Beam shows a short message: "This video is protected, so it can only play here."
