Beam is a Safari Web Extension. The app only shows how to turn the extension on; the feature runs inside Safari.

To test:
1. Open Beam once, then Safari > Settings > Extensions: tick Beam for Safari and allow it on all websites.
2. Open a page with an HTML5 video (for example an MP4 file in a <video> tag), press play, right-click the video and choose "Beam to Apple TV".
3. Safari's AirPlay picker opens. Choosing an Apple TV or other AirPlay receiver on the same network plays the video there.

Without an AirPlay receiver on the network, Beam shows "No Apple TV found. Check that it is on and on the same Wi-Fi network." Beam collects no data and makes no network requests.
