## What we learned (matters for the next steps)

- **Right-click path works:** menu click, one message to the clicked frame, picker opened, on Safari 27.2 / Apple TV. Works in a cross-origin iframe, through an overlay layer, and with both a background page and a service worker.
- **Website permission:** Safari needs per-extension "Allow on websites". Without it the menu item shows and the click silently does nothing. Setup (T008) and the popup (T006) must say so.
- **Sites that switch AirPlay off:** the picker works anyway. Your decision: Beam overrides the opt-out automatically (only on videos that opted out) and has no "Try anyway" button.
- **MediaSource video without an HLS alternate cannot be AirPlayed in this Safari** (Safari's own button fails too). Beam now says so and suggests Screen Mirroring. My first spike conclusion on this was wrong and is corrected in `docs/spike-T002.md`. Lesson: record "picker opened" and "video played on the TV" separately.
- **Protected (DRM) video** is refused with a plain message; Beam never tries to get past DRM.
